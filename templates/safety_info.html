Personal Safety Guidelines

Travel & Commute Safety
  • Share your live location link with a trusted contact before boarding late-night cabs or public transport.
  • Verify vehicle registration plates and driver details before entering a ride-share.
  • Prefer well-lit main roads and stay alert rather than wearing noise-canceling headphones in isolated areas.
Digital & Emergency Preparedness
  • Keep at least 2–3 trusted emergency contacts updated in your profile and speed dial.
  • Use SOS confirmation or dial 112 directly if you feel threatened; move toward crowded areas or open shops.
  • Preserve screenshots, call logs, or dates when reporting harassment or cyberstalking incidents.
